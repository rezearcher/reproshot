# ReproShot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-06_

ReproShot is a browser extension published by Grey Ridge Signals Group LLC.

## The short version

ReproShot does not collect, transmit, or sell your data. Your screenshots, annotations, and bug-report notes stay on your own device. There is no server that receives them.

## What the extension accesses

- **The page you capture.** When you click the ReproShot toolbar button, the extension captures the current tab and reads basic environment facts (the page URL and title, your browser name and version, operating system, viewport and screen size, language, and the time). This happens only after you click, and only on the tab you are viewing. These facts are placed into the capture so you can paste them into a bug report. They are never sent anywhere by ReproShot.
- **Local storage.** The capture is handed from the toolbar to the editor through your browser's local extension storage and is removed once the editor loads. Your selected tool and color preferences are stored locally.
- **Downloads.** When you choose "Download PNG," the extension saves the image you created to your computer.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not upload your screenshots or notes to any server.
- It does not include analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
- It does not read pages in the background. Nothing runs until you click the button.

## Payments

If you choose to upgrade to Pro, the purchase is processed by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which uses Stripe for payment handling. Grey Ridge never receives or stores your card details. ExtensionPay records that your installation is a paying customer so the Pro features unlock. See the ExtensionPay and Stripe privacy policies for how payment data is handled.

## Optional full-page capture

Full-page capture needs permission to scroll the page you are capturing. ReproShot requests that permission only if you turn the feature on, and uses it only to scroll and stitch the capture you asked for.
